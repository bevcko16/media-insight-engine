Patterns across the scenarios:
- Writer B's outputs use more explicit hedging and test designs. A's outputs sometimes add norms or unhedged recommendations: the healthy frequency claim, "rebalance TikTok", "hook-led edits".
- Neither writer consistently checks its findings against the stated objective. Consideration campaigns are judged almost entirely on CTR and CPC.
- Statistical language is loose on both sides. "Overlap heavily", "too small to call" and "consistent with chance" were each stated without a check, and some are wrong.
